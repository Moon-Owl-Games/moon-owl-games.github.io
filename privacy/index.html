Katana Race: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026
This policy is published at https://moon-owl-games.github.io/privacy/. Version française : https://moon-owl-games.github.io/fr/privacy/. In the game, it opens from Settings > ABOUT > PRIVACY POLICY.

Katana Race (the "game") is a free mobile game published by Alexis Fauxbâton (Moon Owl Games) ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game uses, who receives it and what you can do about it.

In short

  • No account and no server of ours. The game never sends us your contact details; to us, it sends only game statistics and crash reports, through providers that process them on our behalf (section 6).
  • Your save stays on your device.
  • Rewarded ads, when you choose to watch one, and at most one ad as the game starts (never on the day of your first launch, at most once every 4 hours, none with the NO ADS purchase). They come from Google AdMob, which receives some data from your device to show and measure them.
  • Your age stays on your device. The game asks it once, to suit the ads to your age; only flags ("child-directed", "under the age of consent") travel with ad requests, never your age.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. Google handles the payment. We never see your card or your Google account.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, ads are personalised only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in the game: DOJO or Settings > ABOUT > PRIVACY.
  • Crash reports (Sentry) and game statistics without a profile (PostHog), for every player, with providers that keep the data in the European Union. No name, no age, no advertising ID, no IP address kept. More detailed statistics only if you agree (in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, at 16 and over). Turn it all off at any time: Settings > STATISTICS.

1. Who is responsible

Alexis Fauxbâton (Moon Owl Games), blmcontactpro1@gmail.com.

We are the controller, in the sense of the GDPR, for the processing described here. For ads, Google also decides how it uses the data for its own purposes, and is responsible for that under its own privacy policy (section 3).

2. What stays on your device

The game saves on your device: your progress (lessons, bosses beaten, runs played, best score and distance, experience and level, missions, the daily and weekly ones included), the outfits and blades you own and wear, your ryo, the daily counters of ad bonuses, try-ons and mission changes, the items you have already seen, the list of store products you own (so you can use them offline), the founder mark, your game settings (sound, vibration, language, NOTIFICATIONS, STATISTICS), your answers to the reminders' question and whether your installation is in their control group, your age (your answer to the first launch's question) and the day of your answer, the day of your first launch, the time of the last ad shown as the game started, and the random identifiers of the statistics (section 6).

  • This save stays in the game's private storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is excluded from Android backups.
  • You can erase it for good by uninstalling the game or with "Clear storage" in the Android settings. We cannot recover it for you.
  • Your purchases are not lost: RESTORE PURCHASES, in the Settings (ABOUT), reads them back from Google Play.

Google's consent tool also keeps on your device your choice on ads and, where it asks you, on the detailed statistics (sections 3 and 6), and the crash-reporting tool (Sentry) keeps there its reports waiting to be sent and a technical file of its own (section 6).

The game's reminders, when they are on (by default on Android 12 and older, otherwise if you accept them), are scheduled on your device, which keeps them until their time (even through a restart) and shows them itself: nothing is sent, to us or to any notification service. They are scheduled when you leave the game and all cancelled when you come back. They arrive around 6 pm, at most one a day: the last day of the weekly mission, if you started it without finishing it; then, counted from the day you left the game, the day's missions 2 days later, your mission under way 4 days later, a reminder 8 days later and a last one 30 days later; after that, nothing. One installation in ten, drawn at random on your device, never gets any (the control group, section 6). Turn them off at any time: Settings > NOTIFICATIONS > OFF, or in the game's Android settings.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game has rewarded ads: you tap a button to watch one, and the reward comes if you watch it to the end. They multiply the ryo of a run, try on an item of the DOJO, change one of the day's missions (once a day) and let you carry on when you lose (the second chance, CONTINUE?, once per run). The game also has one ad as it starts: when you launch the game (not when you come back to it), announced on the loading screen ("AN AD WHILE THE GAME LOADS"), never on the day of your first launch, nor when you open the game from one of its reminders, nor at a launch where the consent form shows, at most once every 4 hours, and only if it is ready within 4 seconds; the NO ADS purchase removes it. No ad shows by itself during a run, no ad is rated above PG, and none above G if you are under 13.

To have an ad ready when you want one, the game loads ads in advance once it starts (after your answer to the age question, and after your choice where consent is asked). So the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game contacts Google even if you never watch an ad. It collects from your device and sends to Google:

  • your IP address, from which Google estimates your approximate location (country, region);
  • identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • information about your device and the game: model, operating system, language, app version;
  • your interactions with ads: shown, tapped, watched to the end;
  • diagnostic data on the performance of the game and of the SDK.

Google uses this data to show ads, limit how often you see the same one, measure them, detect fraud and abuse, and, if you agree, personalise them. The SDK may store and read identifiers on your device, the way a website uses cookies. We do not receive this data, only aggregated reports (number of ads shown, revenue).

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google's privacy policy.

Your age

At the very first launch, before any ad and before the consent form, the game asks your age (an age range, then the age). Your answer stays on your device, with the day you gave it: the game adds a year on each anniversary of that day. It is never sent. Only flags travel with the ad and consent requests:

  • under 13, everywhere: the "child-directed treatment" flag (in the sense of the US COPPA law): Google shows neither personalised ads nor remarketing, and only G-rated ads;
  • under 16 in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland: the "under the age of consent" flag: no consent form, no personalised ads;
  • 16 and over: no flag.

You can change your answer at any time: Settings > AGE. The ads follow at once.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK, Switzerland: if you are 16 or over, before the first ad, Google's consent form asks whether you agree to personalised ads and to the use of identifiers on your device. If you refuse, the game works the same; you may see non-personalised or limited ads, which use data only to deliver and count them and to fight fraud. Change your choice at any time: DOJO or Settings > ABOUT > PRIVACY.
  • Everywhere: in the Android settings (usually Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads), you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation. You can also manage Google ads at My Ad Center.

4. Purchases (Google Play)

What is sold for real money (outfits and blades, sets, and the NO ADS purchase) is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment under the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Payments Privacy Notice. We never see your card, your name, your email address or your Google account.

The game receives from Google Play only which product you bought, whether it is pending or paid, whether it is already confirmed, and a purchase token. It uses them on your device to unlock your items, confirm the purchase to Google Play, and remember what you own.

In our Google Play Console, Google shows us the order records (order number, product, date, price, country). We use them only for refunds, support and our accounting.

5. What Google Play shows us

Like every developer, we see in the Google Play Console statistics about installs, your public review if you write one, and technical crash reports that Google collects from devices whose owners agreed to share diagnostics with Google. These come from Google Play, not from the game, and do not include your name or contact details.

6. Crash reports and statistics

To fix bugs and improve the game, the game sends crash reports and game statistics to two providers that process them on our behalf only (processors, in the sense of the GDPR, bound to us by a data processing agreement). None of it is used for ads or to identify you. The game sends them encrypted (TLS). Sentry and PostHog see the IP address of your connection, like any server that receives data: we have set them not to keep it.

Turn it all off at any time: Settings > STATISTICS > OFF. Nothing more is sent, crash reports included, until you set it back to ON, and the statistics identifiers are erased from your device. Nothing that happens in the meantime is sent afterwards, not even a crash.

Crash reports (Sentry)

When the game hits an error or crashes, it sends a report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), which keeps its data in the European Union (Germany). A report contains: the error message, the sequence of the game's functions that led to it (with a few lines of the game's code), and for a crash, the technical state of the game at that moment; the game's last errors before this one; the version of the game, of its engine and of the system; the device model, its processor, memory, storage and screen, its battery state and connection type; the device's language and time zone; and the latest changes of state of the game and the device (game in the foreground or not, battery, connection). Sentry also counts the game's launches with and without a crash, with a random identifier that is new at each launch. A report contains neither your name, nor your age, nor an advertising or device identifier, nor a screenshot, nor anything from your save.

These reports are sent for every player, without consent, on the basis of our legitimate interest in finding and fixing the game's errors (section 8), and you can object at any time (STATISTICS). A crash is sent at the next launch.

Statistics without a profile (PostHog)

The game sends to PostHog (PostHog Inc.), in its service hosted in the European Union (Germany), game events: launching and coming back to the game; the start of each run (its number, the lessons you have succeeded at so far) and its end (defeat or quitting, distance, score, duration, bosses beaten, difficulty reached, scenery, what defeated you and with which attack); missions completed; ryo earned (and from what) and spent (and on what); opening the DOJO, items viewed, tried on, equipped and unlocked; ads offered, started, watched to the end or not (closed early, or not shown, and why); the second chance (offered, taken, declined and how, granted); purchases started, paid, cancelled or failed (the product, its displayed price and its currency, never an order number or a token); the reminders' question (shown, your answer, Android's), the reminders scheduled and opened (their kind, never their text) and the NOTIFICATIONS setting. With each: its date and time, the game's version, the system (Android), the game's language, the number of days since your first launch, the number of runs played, your level, and whether your installation is in the reminders' control group (one in ten, drawn at random on your device, which never gets any: to measure what they change).

These statistics make no profile of you. They carry a random identifier of your installation, created by the game, unrelated to your identity, your device or other apps, and replaced by a new one 12 months after its creation (or when you turn STATISTICS off and on again). Each event also carries the days since your first launch, the number of runs played, your level and the country set in your device's region settings (for example "FR"): never your location, nor your IP address. We only look at their totals and averages (for example, how many players come back the next day, where the first runs stop). We combine them with no other data and pass them on to no one. As the CNIL (the French data protection authority) allows for audience measurement limited this way, they do not require your consent; they rely on our legitimate interest in improving the game (section 8), and you can object at any time (STATISTICS).

Detailed statistics, with your agreement

If you agree, the same events are sent, from your agreement on (earlier ones stay without a profile), with a pseudonymous identifier, created by the game and kept on your device, that does not change, and PostHog builds a profile from it: the history of your runs in the game, to understand how players progress over time. Nothing else is sent: no name, no age, no advertising ID.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: only at 16 and over, and only if you accept, in Google's consent form, our purposes "Store and/or access information on a device", "Measure content performance", "Understand audiences through statistics or combinations of data from different sources" and "Develop and improve services". Otherwise, the statistics stay without a profile. Change your choice at any time: DOJO or Settings > ABOUT > PRIVACY, or STATISTICS > OFF.
  • Elsewhere: at 13 and over.
  • Never under 13, nor before your answer to the age question.

7. If you write to us

If you write to blmcontactpro1@gmail.com, we use your email address and your message only to answer you. We keep them 3 years after our last exchange, then delete them.

8. Why we may use this data (legal bases)

  • The save on your device: needed to provide the game you asked for.
  • Personalised ads, and identifiers stored or read on your device where the law requires consent: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time (DOJO or Settings > ABOUT > PRIVACY).
  • Your age, on your device, and the age flags of ad requests: complying with the rules that protect minors (a legal obligation), and our legitimate interest in not showing them unsuitable ads.
  • Non-personalised ads where no consent is required, ad measurement, fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding a free game with ads, and Google's in protecting its ad network.
  • Purchases: delivering what you bought.
  • Crash reports: our legitimate interest in finding and fixing the game's errors, with minimal data and no lasting identifier; you can object (Settings > STATISTICS).
  • Statistics without a profile: our legitimate interest in improving the game; the random identifier they keep on your device falls under the consent exemption for audience measurement (article 82 of the French Data Protection Act, CNIL guidelines); you can object (Settings > STATISTICS).
  • Detailed statistics: your consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, which you can withdraw at any time (DOJO or Settings > ABOUT > PRIVACY, or STATISTICS); elsewhere, our legitimate interest in improving the game.
  • Answering your messages: our legitimate interest in replying to you.

9. Who receives data

  • Google, for ads and purchases: Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and Switzerland, Google LLC elsewhere, including the UK. Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States; for transfers from Europe it relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (with its UK and Swiss extensions) and on standard contractual clauses.
  • Our processors, which process the data on our behalf only, under our instructions, without reusing it: Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) for crash reports, and PostHog (PostHog Inc.) for statistics. Both keep the game's data in the European Union; they are US companies, and any access from the United States (their support, their own sub-processors) is covered by their data processing agreement (the European Commission's standard contractual clauses).

We do not sell your data, and we share nothing with anyone else.

10. How long data is kept

  • Your save, your age included: on your device, until you delete it.
  • Ad data: by Google, under its own retention rules (for example, Google anonymises advertising data in its server logs by removing part of the IP address after 9 months and cookie information after 18 months).
  • Purchase records: by Google, under its own legal obligations.
  • Crash reports: by Sentry, 30 days.
  • Statistics: by PostHog, 1 year (if our PostHog plan changes this period, never more than 25 months). The identifier of the statistics without a profile is replaced 12 months after its creation; the one of the detailed statistics stays on your device until you turn STATISTICS off or uninstall the game.
  • Your emails: 3 years after our last exchange.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR in the EEA, UK GDPR, Swiss law, California law...), you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw your consent at any time. In France, you can also give instructions on what happens to your data after your death.

We hold nothing that lets us recognise you, except the emails you send us: we do not know which random statistics identifier is yours, and cannot find your data without it (article 11 of the GDPR). So most of these rights are exercised directly:

  • your save: on your device (section 2);
  • your age: Settings > AGE;
  • crash reports and statistics: Settings > STATISTICS > OFF, which stops all sending and erases the statistics identifiers from your device; for the detailed statistics, also DOJO or Settings > ABOUT > PRIVACY;
  • ad data: DOJO or Settings > ABOUT > PRIVACY, the Android ad settings, or your Google account;
  • purchases: in Google Play.

For anything else, write to blmcontactpro1@gmail.com. We answer within one month.

You may also complain to a data protection authority: in France, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr, 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07); in the UK, the ICO (ico.org.uk); in Switzerland, the FDPIC; elsewhere in the EEA, the authority of your country.

12. Children

Katana Race is intended for players 13 and over and is not part of Google Play's Families program. We do not knowingly collect data about children. At the first launch, the game asks your age, suggesting nothing (section 3): under 13, its ad requests are marked "child-directed" (no personalised ads, only G-rated ads); under 16 in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, they are marked "under the age of consent", and the consent form is not offered to you (the age at which you may consent alone is between 13 and 16 in Europe, 15 in France; the game applies 16 everywhere). Your age never leaves your device. Crash reports and statistics without a profile (section 6) apply to every player: they carry no name, no age and no advertising ID; detailed statistics never under 13, nor under 16 in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland. A parent who believes their child has used the game can write to us and we will help.

13. Security

We have no server and no database of our own. Crash reports and statistics are held by Sentry and PostHog, in protected accounts that only we can access. Your save stays in the game's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Data sent to Google, Sentry and PostHog is encrypted in transit (TLS).

14. California and other US states

We do not sell your personal information for money. Personalised ads by Google may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law. To opt out, delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in the Android settings (section 3).

The personal information involved is: identifiers (IP address, advertising ID; and the random statistics identifiers), approximate location (inferred from the IP address, by Google only), internet or other electronic network activity (your interactions with ads; your actions in the game, for the statistics), commercial information (your purchases, held by Google; the product and price of a purchase, in the statistics) and technical information (crash reports). Statistics and crash reports are neither sold nor shared. Google may collect information about your activity over time and across apps to personalise ads. The game has no web browser and does not respond to "Do Not Track" signals; the Android settings above do that job.

15. Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it uses data, for example if we add cloud saves or accounts, we will update this policy before that version comes out, change the date at the top, and tell you in the game or in the update notes when the change matters to you.
