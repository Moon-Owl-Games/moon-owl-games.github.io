Moon Owl Games presents

Katana Race

An action runner for phones, played in landscape with two thumbs. A run lasts one to three minutes.

  • Tap left to jump.
  • Tap right to slash, or to parry on time.
  • Chain your cuts: the blade catches fire as the pace climbs, and a boss bars the road about every 30 seconds.
  • Outfits and blades to unlock at the Dojo.
